PHYD57: Advanced Computational Methods in Physics

Welcome to the course that will help you become a more knowledgeable and better scientific programmer!

PHYD57: Advanced Computational Methods in Physics

Fall 2025 | University of Toronto Scarborough

This page provides the sole access to all the materials, up-to-date SYLLABUS, texts of assignments, and preliminary grades. Quercus will be used for: submission of your term work, announcements via email, and posting of recordings.

📝 Brief Intro

You already know basic programming in Python. Now is the time to learn some history and physics behind our technical civilization, where computing is helping us everywhere. Powerful operating systems (Linux) and HPC languages (C/C++, Fortran) will be discussed and tried out, along with networking and the principles of parallel programming (OpenMP, CUDA) that enable large-scale physical simulations.

  • Tutorials: Immediately following lectures (T1..T10), 1 hr.

  • Deadlines: Sets of assignments/projects (A1-A4) are due at 11 am on specific dates.
