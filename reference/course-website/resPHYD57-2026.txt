Exerpts from UofT rules: 
  A UofT student can use the ACORN Absence Declaration (AD) tool ONCE PER 
  ACADEMIC TERM for a single continuous period of up to 7 days. More 
  extended absences require Verification of Illenss form (VOI). This 
  declaration can cover multiple courses. After an absence declaration 
  is submitted, it cannot be cancelled. It can be modified until the end
  of submission day. (...) If you have already submitted AD and have  
  another absence, you may be asked for supporting documentation (VOI).
  These aditional requests beyond the 1st AD will be considered at the
  discretion of instructors.
PHYD57 rules: 
  Medical forms from actual in-person visits in T.O. will be considered. 
  Example: Ineligible are notes from Hamilton-area doctors sold online
  for $49.99. 
  You are asked to submit VOI for all absences other than the 1st
  self-declared one. If, for any reason, you do not submit at least one 
  half of the term work (assignments + midterm) then no more transfers 
  will be offered; you will then be judged unlikely to be able to pass
  the course successfully, and at that point you should consider 
  withdrawing before the academic penalty deadline.
